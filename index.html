Flight Dynamics Breakdown

1. Vertical Climb: Increasing all 4 motors produces net thrust greater than the drone's weight ($T > mg$).

2. Pitch Forward: Speeding up M1 & M3 (rear) while slowing M2 & M4 (front) tilts the thrust vector backward, driving the craft forward.

3. Torque Yaw: Speeding up CW motors while slowing CCW motors creates an aerodynamic torque imbalance, rotating the fuselage without climbing.

Safety Checklist

  • Smoke Stopper Check: Always plug in a current-limiting smoke stopper before your first LiPo connection.
  • Props OFF on Bench: Never connect a battery with propellers attached inside the workshop.
  • Low-ESR Capacitor: Solder a 35V 1000uF capacitor across the battery pads to absorb active braking voltage spikes.
